Professional Profile

Integrity, Diligence & Diligent Legal Advocacy

Advocate Govind J. Prajapati (BLS / LL.B) is an established legal practitioner and consultant practicing before the High Court of Bombay, City Civil & Sessions Courts, Family Courts, and key statutory redevelopment authorities across Maharashtra.

The practice handles civil suits, bail and criminal defense, commercial litigations, matrimonial disputes, property title drafting, and redevelopment matters (SRA/MHADA/BMC), founded on deep procedural preparation and uncompromising ethics.
